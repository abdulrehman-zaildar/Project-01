Quick Energy Saving Tips

❄️

AC Optimization

Set AC to 26°C instead of 18°C. Each degree lower adds ~6% to your cooling bill.

💡

Switch to LED

LED bulbs use 75% less energy than incandescent bulbs and last 25x longer.

🔌

Phantom Load

Unplug chargers and devices when not in use. Standby power wastes 5-10% of your bill.

⏰

Off-Peak Hours

Run heavy appliances (iron, washing machine) during off-peak hours to save on tariff.

🌡️

Fridge Efficiency

Keep fridge at 3-5°C and freezer at -18°C. Don't put hot food directly in.

☀️

Natural Light

Use daylight as much as possible. Open curtains instead of turning on lights.
